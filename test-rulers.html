Testing Rulers Feature

1. Open http://localhost:3000

2. Click the hamburger menu (☰) in the top-left corner

3. Look for "Toggle rulers" option in the menu

4. Click it to enable/disable rulers

5. When enabled, you should see rulers along the top and left edges of the canvas

What you should see:

  • A menu item with ruler icon and "Toggle rulers" text
  • When toggled on: rulers appear along the top and left borders
  • Rulers show measurement marks and scale with the canvas
  • The state persists when you refresh the page
